About ClassCycle

ClassCycle is a site that facilitates the resale of gently used school supplies by connecting sellers and buyers. No longer used or unused supplies such as textbooks can be resold at lower prices to people who need them. We aim to decrease the financial barrier of scholastic success and improve the accessibility of education worldwide.

This project was built for the Congressional App Challenge by Jiaqi Li.
